# Tasso, Inc.

> Tasso, Inc. makes painless, needle-free blood collection devices (Tasso+, Tasso-M20, Tasso-SST / tile-T20) paired with an end-to-end logistics platform for decentralized and at-home blood sampling used in clinical trials, patient screening, and research. Tasso is cited in over 100 peer-reviewed publications. No public developer API surface is currently published.
